Traffic Ahead per Lap

All 59 competitors. Dot colour = our rider (blue=Bálint, red=Loris, green=Máté, yellow=Jan). White dot = overtake happened that lap. Large coloured dot = strict catch-up (pace diff > gap). Small fading dot = approaching (opacity = proximity).

How it's calculated: Each dot = one competitor within range during one of our laps. Dot colour = our rider. Paces = per-team stint median (each team's own rider stints), laps within 5% of stint fastest. Hold-up = competitor's pace slower than ours AND pace advantage > current gap. White dot = gap ≤ 1.5 s then reset ≥ 5 s (position change). Excluded: pit laps, out-laps, SC periods (yellow bands), laps with no timing data.

EWC timing · sector analysis

Traffic Ahead — Sector Resolution

Each lap produces 3 dots (S1 · S2 · S3). Dot colour = our rider. Approach/hold-up/white logic identical to lap chart.

How it's calculated: Gap evolves through each sector: gap after Sk = gap before Sk − (competitor Sk − our Sk). Sector paces = per-team stint median by sector, filtered to laps within 5% of stint fastest. A cluster of 3 large dots = held up the full lap. Only S1 large = held up in the opening sector only. Excluded: laps where sector data is unavailable, SC periods (yellow bands), laps >5% slower than stint pace (yellow flags / slow zones). Le Mans uses 3 sectors — S1 ≈ 44 s (pit straight + chicanes), S2 ≈ 27 s (Ford chicanes + Dunlop), S3 ≈ 26 s (Porsche + Maison Blanche).
